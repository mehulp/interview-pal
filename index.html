3. Response and devices

Respond by

    Tip: a headset microphone close to your mouth works much better than the laptop's built-in one. The test only measures loudness: nothing is recorded, transcribed or sent.

    The preview is only on this screen and stops when you leave setup or start. Allowing the camera for a preview does not record video: that is the Recording choice below.

    Recording

    Not remembered: Recording is Off every time you open setup, unless you choose it here for this session. Nothing is recorded until you press Start answer. Audio is saved only if you press Save; video is only ever downloaded.

    More options

    Speech recognition is done by your browser. In Chrome it normally sends your speech to Google, so Practice is not guaranteed to work offline. Typing never sends anything.

    Tip: place this window so the interviewer's eyes sit just below your webcam, and look at them while you answer.

        Microphone check
        
      
        
        Speech recognition works best with a clear, steady voice level. Press the button and speak normally for a few seconds.
        It shows which microphone is in use and how loud you are. Nothing is recorded.
        It tests the microphone chosen on the Interview screen.
      
        
      
      
        
      
        
      
          

      
          Test it
          
      
          Sends one tiny request to Anthropic to check that the key and model work. It costs a fraction of a cent.
          
      
      
          

      
          
        Anyone who can open this browser profile can read the saved key, so do not use a shared computer.
        You can also set a monthly spend limit for the key in the Anthropic Console.
